# Party Combat Idea

## Status

This folder contains an early combat concept for a game in which the player controls a party. It is a discussion draft, not a final team decision.

The main design goal is to create tactical depth through party composition, character roles, equipment, skills, and the order in which player and enemy units act. An MVP could begin with two playable characters, while a later version could expand toward the original four-character idea.

## Current Direction

The player controls multiple characters during combat. Each character can have selectable active skills, character-bound Inherent Skills, weapons, armor, and accessories. Skills may require specific weapon types, while damage and defense may use categories such as Slashing, Blunt, Fire, or Lightning.

Two turn-order models are still being considered:

1. The entire player side acts, followed by the enemy side.
2. All player and enemy units act according to Speed, either once per round or on a continuous timeline that may allow faster units to gain extra turns.
